# Case Study: Wastage & Leftover Analytics

## Objective

Assess whether material wastage is within approved standards and identify periods requiring investigation.

## Core Measures

Actual Wastage % = Actual Wastage Kg / Input Kg

Excess Wastage = Actual Wastage Kg - (Input Kg x Standard Wastage %)

## Audit Perspective

A variance above the standard is an exception for investigation, not automatically evidence of loss or misconduct. The reviewer should consider material type, production complexity, approved technical allowances and documented operational reasons.

## Management Outputs

- Monthly wastage trend
- Standard versus actual comparison
- Excess wastage by production area
- Leftover ageing
- Disposal and recovery analysis
- Reconciliation exception register
