# Finance & Accounting Analytics

## Objective

Demonstrate how accounting and operational data can be converted into management information for performance review, cost control and decision support.

## Focus Areas

- Budget versus actual analysis
- Cost variance analysis
- Revenue and margin analysis
- Working capital analysis
- Receivables and payables ageing
- Expense trend analysis
- General ledger review
- Account reconciliation
- Financial ratio analysis
- Management reporting

## Example KPI Categories

**Profitability** → Gross margin · Operating margin · Contribution margin

**Liquidity** → Current ratio · Quick ratio · Cash conversion indicators

**Working Capital** → Receivable days · Inventory days · Payable days

**Cost Control** → Budget variance · Cost per unit · Expense growth

## Management Reporting Approach

1. Validate source data
2. Define reporting period and scope
3. Analyse trend and variance
4. Investigate material exceptions
5. Identify business drivers
6. Present concise management insights
7. Track corrective actions

## Tools

Excel · Power Query · Oracle ERP · MIS · Power BI concepts

> **Confidentiality note:** This repository does not contain actual employer financial statements or confidential accounting records.
