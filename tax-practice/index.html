Return preparation

From source documents to return submission

A practical workflow I use as a framework for demonstrating the logic of individual tax return preparation.

01Collect
Salary, bank, investment, property and other income records.
02Classify
Map income, exempt items, allowable items and supporting evidence.
03Calculate
Aggregate taxable income and apply the relevant tax structure.
04Reconcile
Match TDS, advance tax and eligible tax credits with records.
05File & retain
Prepare the return, submit through the applicable process and retain evidence.
Control mindset

Tax return review checklist

✓Income sources agree with supporting records.
✓TDS / advance tax agrees with certificates and payment records.
✓Tax rebate or credit claims have supporting evidence.
✓Assets, liabilities and relevant disclosures are internally consistent.
✓Arithmetic and reconciliation differences are investigated before submission.
Why it belongs here

Finance + Tax + Analytics

This project demonstrates the same approach used in audit and finance analytics: gather evidence, classify transactions, calculate the expected result, reconcile independent records and investigate exceptions.
