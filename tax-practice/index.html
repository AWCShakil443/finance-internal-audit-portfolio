An interactive, illustrative case study demonstrating income aggregation, taxpayer-category thresholds, progressive tax calculation, minimum tax, business turnover tax, TDS reconciliation and the workflow behind individual income tax return preparation.

Interactive tool

Illustrative Bangladesh Personal Tax Calculator

This calculator is based on the structure and terminology of the supplied Assessment Year 2026–2027 workbook. It now captures taxpayer category, minimum tax and the requested 1% turnover-tax test for business income instead of treating every taxpayer as a general male taxpayer.

Mandatory checks added

Tax calculation is more than applying a slab

01 · Taxpayer category

Gender is captured because the supplied workbook uses different tax-free thresholds for male, female and third-gender taxpayers.

02 · Minimum tax

The calculator applies the workbook's reference minimum of Tk. 1,000 for a first return and Tk. 5,000 otherwise, subject to the business turnover test below.

03 · Business turnover tax

Where business turnover is entered, a 1% turnover-tax amount is calculated and compared with the other tax floor in the illustrative liability calculation.

04 · Disabled child

A Tk. 50,000 additional threshold is included when the taxpayer indicates a disabled child, consistent with the supplied workbook.

05 · Physically challenged

The taxpayer status is captured as a return-preparation field so the case can be reviewed separately for applicable treatment.

06 · Freedom Fighter

The category is included because the supplied workbook provides a separate threshold for Freedom Fighter status.

Return preparation

From source documents to return submission

A practical workflow for demonstrating individual tax return preparation with evidence, classification and reconciliation controls.

01Collect
Salary, bank, investment, property, business and other income records.
02Classify
Map income, exempt items, allowable expenses, deductions and supporting evidence.
03Calculate
Aggregate taxable income and apply taxpayer category, slabs, minimum tax and business turnover test.
04Reconcile
Match TDS, advance tax and eligible tax credits with certificates and payment records.
05File & retain
Prepare the return, submit through the applicable process and retain evidence.
Control mindset

Tax return review checklist

✓Income sources agree with supporting records.
✓Taxpayer category and applicable threshold are correctly selected.
✓Business turnover is separately captured where business income exists.
✓Minimum tax and other applicable tax floors are considered.
✓TDS / advance tax agrees with certificates and payment records.
✓Tax rebate or credit claims have supporting evidence.
✓Assets, liabilities and relevant disclosures are internally consistent.
✓Arithmetic and reconciliation differences are investigated before submission.
